## Narrative and pacing

- **S01–S02, 2:00:** Establish the practical task and invite curiosity about the first response.
- **S03–S05, 3:10:** Give names to visible pieces and explain shared service through one analogy.
- **S06–S08, 3:50:** Walk through this project's intended mechanism and verification step.
- **S09–S11, 3:45:** Explore limits, responsibility, evidence, and how usefulness will be evaluated.
- **S12, 1:15:** Return to the original task and let the audience explain the relationships.

All exact audience copy and source IDs are in the [slide plan](slide-plan.md). Visual IDs resolve in [visual briefs](visual-briefs.md); source and claim IDs resolve in [evidence](evidence.md).

## Vocabulary progression

“Question” and “manual” precede “app.” “Messages” precedes “chatbot.” “Shared technology” precedes “Bot as a Service.” “Supporting passage” precedes “citation.” Avoid RAG, APIs, JWTs, Entra, Flask, and iframe in the core slides. An optional appendix can name them after the audience understands the pieces.

## Delivery choices

Show the response on S02 only after the audience predicts it. Reveal the preparation lane on S07 before the question lane. Pause on S08 before highlighting the supporting passage. Each reveal must also work as a static, fully visible slide for printed or reduced-motion viewing.

If discussion expands, reduce the library analogy discussion and the responsibility-table narration. Keep the source-check explanation, live-evidence limitation, and final comprehension check. Do not accelerate the talk by shrinking text or omitting qualification of an intended behavior.
